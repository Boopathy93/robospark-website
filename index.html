Inside a 90-minute class

Learn it, build it, break it, fix it

Discover

A short demo or question starts the idea: why does a pulley make lifting easier?

Build

Teams of 2–3 put the project together with real parts and tools.

Test & fix

It rarely works first time. Finding the fault is where the real learning happens.

Show & tell

Each team explains what they built and records it in their maker journal.
